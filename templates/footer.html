Report Content

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Found an Issue?

ClipVault aggregates content from third-party sources. If you encounter content that is:

  • Copyright infringement — your work used without permission
  • Inappropriate or harmful — violates our acceptable use standards
  • Misattributed — wrong source or author credited
  • Broken — preview or download link doesn't work

How to Report

To report content, please include the following in your report:

  1. Clip ID or URL — the exact clip you're reporting
  2. Source — which platform it came from (Pexels, Pixabay, etc.)
  3. Issue description — what's wrong and why
  4. Your relationship — are you the rights holder, or a user reporting an issue?

Copyright Claims

For copyright infringement claims, please include:

  • Proof of ownership or authorization to act on behalf of the rights holder
  • Identification of the copyrighted work
  • A statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized

Submit a Report

Email reports to: report@clipvault.local

We review all reports within 48 hours. If the content violates our terms or the source's license, we will remove it from search results immediately.

Note: ClipVault does not host content. If your report concerns content hosted on Pexels, Pixabay, or another source, we recommend also reporting it directly to that platform.
